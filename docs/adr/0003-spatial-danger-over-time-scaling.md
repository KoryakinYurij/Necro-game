# Make spatial danger primary over elapsed-time scaling

Exploration Mode will derive its baseline danger from space and authored encounter composition rather than using elapsed run time as the primary global scaler. Player power may provide a soft correction and future world-reactivity may add secondary pressure, but time must not silently recreate the old Arena Director under another name.

## Consequences

Encounter composition, POI risk, and reward tier should communicate why one direction is more dangerous than another. Pure HP inflation is not sufficient evidence of spatial danger.
